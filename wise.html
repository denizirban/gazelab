03 / Build the research moat

WISE research roadmap

Phase 1 · Validate

Standardize calibration, holdout error, head-pose drift, repeatability and explicit quality thresholds. Compare webcam results against a reference eye tracker.

Phase 2 · Connect

Add consented gaze samples to visual search tasks. Compare behavioral-only predictions with gaze-plus-behavior predictions.

Phase 3 · Deploy

Offer a researcher workspace for defined areas of interest, anonymized aggregate comparisons, and exportable study reports. Validate a paid pilot before developing hardware.

Product principle

Do not label estimated gaze as measured attention, or attention as understanding. No health or diagnostic claims.
